Verity Impact

Founder and operating partner 2025 to present

I work across a portfolio of early stage companies, which have collectively raised over $100M of venture capital. Some of it is advisory: sitting alongside founder-CEOs on the strategic challenges that are keeping them up, whether that’s the fundraise, the plan behind it, or defining what the company actually is. Some of it is closer in. I also partner with technical founders at the pre-seed stage to spin technologies out of university labs, which is the same lab-to-market problem I lived at Huue. The other thread running through all of the work these days is AI, which I build into my own workflows and help clients embed into theirs.

Skills: university spinout and IP strategy · fundraising strategy, pitch narrative, and financial modeling · strategic operating plans · market analysis and commercialization strategy · leadership coaching and people operations · AI-enabled operations

Huue

CEO and co-founder 2019 to 2024

I was the only commercial and operating person in a deeply technical company, which meant every function that wasn’t science was mine to build: the vision, the fundraising, the customers, the hiring, the finances, the culture, the hard conversations. I raised $25M across multiple rounds through a volatile market, closed our first commercial partnerships with global brands, and led engineering-commercial programs that resulted in a product that outperformed the biological incumbent by 2x and a scaled manufacturing process to support customer delivery. Along the way Huue was named a TIME Best Invention and a World Economic Forum Technology Pioneer, and I was the company’s external voice on stage at SXSW and CES. I loved the specific work of supporting technical talent to flourish, giving brilliant scientists the context, the resources, and the clarity to do the thing only they can do.

Skills: fundraising and board management · goal setting and operating rhythms · org design and people leadership · financial management · restructuring · manufacturing scale-up · brand and marketing · go-to-market and pricing strategy · commercial partnerships and deal structuring

Yelp

Business operations and strategy 2017 to 2019

I chose Yelp deliberately. I wanted to move into tech, and I wanted to work with a world-class business operations and analytics team, because I’d decided data literacy was critical in the toolkit. I honed my SQL, Python, and Tableau skills and used that fluency to help analytics drive the rest of the business. Most of that work sat with product and sales leadership during the launch of the self-serve business model. I ran the quantitative analysis behind B2B marketing promotions and led cross-functional initiatives with the marketing department. It’s also where I first leaned into the people-oriented side of leading, running professional development and designing the team’s feedback system.

Skills: marketing and pricing analytics · sales compensation design · cross-functional program leadership · business model launch · SQL, Python, Tableau
