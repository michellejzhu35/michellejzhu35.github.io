I work with mission-driven deep tech and technology startups, usually alongside a technical founder who's passionate about the product and science and wants a partner for everything else. For six years, I did that as CEO and co-founder of Huue, which started as a UC Berkeley PhD thesis and ended up a VC-backed color company, raising $25M over multiple rounds. I took the core technology license out of the university, built the go-to-market from scratch, closed the first contracts, and built the team and culture across R&D, operations, and commercial. It was my MBA and PhD at once; I learned by doing, and because I'm someone who is constantly reflecting on how to do a thing better, I came out of it with actual frameworks and principles for the initiatives I took on (in addition to a few fun war stories).

Before Huue, I developed my business fundamentals in strategy consulting at Deloitte and business operations at Yelp. Working with Fortune 500 companies gave me experience across the P&L, data fluency, and the habit of learning new things fast. That made me a generalist who could be dropped into any situation. And while that still helps me onboard any project and company quickly, I've since developed a specialization of sorts in defining company vision and narrative, fundraising strategy, board preparation, strategic planning, and the operating cadence that connects all of it.

Because I've sat in the founder-CEO seat, I can meet founders where they actually are. I know how taxing things are when the technology is uncertain and money is finite, and how much courage it takes to choose to keep moving forward. I know what it feels like to have a hundred competing priorities, so I aim to be the most strategic conversation you have all week. I'm at my best rolling up my sleeves on the hard, unglamorous work: strategic operating plans, pitch decks, financial models, org design, AI implementation. I'm also unapologetically values-driven about how I work, which has never meant lowering the bar. The companies I want to help build are the ones solving the hard problems worth solving. That's why I put care into both how a company treats its people and whether it hits its numbers — in fact, I fundamentally believe the two are linked.

Off the clock, I'm a mom of two, and I love being a working mom. I aim to parent by modeling what it looks like to feel purposeful in what you do. And some days I'm just surviving the day-to-day.

Huue

CEO and co-founder 2019 to 2024

I was the only commercial and operating person in a deeply technical company, which meant every function that wasn't science was mine to build: the vision, the fundraising, the customers, the hiring, the finances, the culture, the hard conversations. I raised $25M across multiple rounds through a volatile market, closed our first commercial partnerships with global brands, took a product from ambiguous customer signal to outperforming the incumbent by 2x, and ran a 50x manufacturing scale-up that cut unit cost 92% in a year. Along the way Huue was named a TIME Best Invention and a World Economic Forum Technology Pioneer, and I was the company's external voice on stage at SXSW and CES. What I didn't expect was how much I would love the specific work of supporting technical talent to flourish, giving brilliant scientists the context, the resources, and the clarity to do the thing only they can do.

Skills: fundraising and board management · goal setting and operating rhythms · org design and people leadership · financial management · restructuring · manufacturing scale-up · brand and marketing · go-to-market and pricing strategy · commercial partnerships and deal structuring

Yelp

Business operations and strategy 2017 to 2019

I chose Yelp deliberately. I wanted to move into tech, and I wanted to work with a world-class business operations and analytics team, because I'd decided data literacy was critical in the toolkit. I honed my SQL, Python, and Tableau skills and used that fluency to help analytics drive the rest of the business. Most of that work sat with product and sales leadership during the launch of the self-serve business model. I ran the quantitative analysis behind B2B marketing promotions and led cross-functional initiatives with the marketing department. It's also where I first leaned into the people-oriented side of leading, running professional development and designing the team's feedback system.

Skills: marketing and pricing analytics · sales compensation design · cross-functional program leadership · business model launch · SQL, Python, Tableau
